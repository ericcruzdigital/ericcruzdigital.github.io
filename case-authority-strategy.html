Off-page SEO · Professional strategy

From citation volume to an authority-first SEO plan

For a logistics client, I proposed replacing a generic citation-heavy approach with a tighter off-page strategy tied to geography, services, buyer intent, and useful authority assets.

Local SEO · Off-page SEO · Buyer intent · Authority · Cross-channel alignment

Problem

More placements were not automatically better.

The existing direction focused heavily on citation production. I wanted the off-page work to connect more clearly to the client’s actual market: local relevance, high-value services, and the buyers the business wanted to attract.

Strategy

Build authority around two useful themes.

Local authority

Strengthen geographic relevance with consistent service-area language, accurate business information, appropriate local listings, and locally relevant supporting assets.

Industry and buyer authority

Create useful content and placements around the specific services and buyer problems the company wanted to grow, instead of broad generic visibility.

Fewer, better placementsPrioritize relevance, credibility, and fit over raw submission count.
Useful supporting assetsCreate focused resources that can support both discovery and off-page authority.
Channel alignmentUse paid-search audience insights to sharpen organic buyer targeting where appropriate.
ConsistencyKeep location, services, business details, and destination pages aligned across placements.
Modern search

AI search does not change the need for credibility.

My current approach is to build useful, crawlable, original content and real authority signals rather than chase special markup or artificial mentions. Community participation only makes sense when the answer is genuinely useful and the audience fit is real.

That keeps the off-page strategy compatible with both traditional search and newer AI-powered discovery without turning it into a separate bag of tricks.

Execution

A repeatable cycle

  1. Choose one narrow service or buyer problem.
  2. Create or improve one useful authority asset.
  3. Select a small set of relevant listings or placements.
  4. Check consistency, destination quality, and audience fit.
  5. Review visibility and refine the next cycle.

Client identity and private campaign details are excluded from this public version.
