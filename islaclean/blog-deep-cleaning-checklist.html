Room-by-room deep cleaning checklist

A deep clean is easier when the work is divided by room and by type of task. Start high, finish low, and leave floors until the end.

Before you start

  • Clear obvious clutter so surfaces are accessible.
  • Gather cloths, brushes, a mop or vacuum, and suitable cleaning products.
  • Work from dry dust removal toward wet cleaning.
  • Test products on a small area when the surface is unfamiliar.

Kitchen

  • Dust tops of cabinets and reachable ledges.
  • Wipe cabinet faces, handles, and commonly touched surfaces.
  • Clean counters, backsplash, and sink area.
  • Finish with the floor after upper surfaces are complete.

Bathrooms

  • Work on mirrors, fixtures, and visible buildup.
  • Pay attention to edges and frequently touched areas.
  • Clean floor areas last to avoid re-soiling.

Bedrooms and living areas

  • Dust furniture, shelves, and accessible ledges.
  • Wipe doors, switches, and high-touch surfaces.
  • Vacuum or sweep before mopping where appropriate.

When a deep clean makes sense

If the home mainly needs ongoing upkeep, routine house cleaning is the clearer fit. If the goal is a more detailed reset, see the deep-cleaning service page. For an empty property or handover, compare move-in and move-out cleaning.
