Tools & working context
Google Search Console, GA4, Ubersuggest, Looker Studio, Brevo and Google Sheets

Context

A metric needs context.

A ranking movement, traffic change or email open count is not a complete explanation. Comparisons need the right dates, source and campaign context before they can support a recommendation.

I check the data, investigate unusual changes and write a concise finding with an appropriate follow-up.

My process

From observation to action.

  1. Confirm the comparison

    Check the date range, source and comparison period. Confirm what is actually being measured before summarizing movement.

  2. Read the signals together

    Use search performance, website behavior and campaign activity as context. Keep an enquiry, click and sale distinct.

  3. Investigate before concluding

    Review tracking gaps, unusual traffic, ranking or indexing issues and page relevance when a result needs attention.

  4. Write the next action

    Explain the verified observation, relevant limitation and recommended follow-up. Record the owner or input needed to continue.
