What I analyze

Signals across the customer journey.

I look across channels instead of treating each report as an isolated number.

Acquisition

Where users are coming from, how channels are contributing, and which traffic sources are changing.

Engagement

How users interact with important pages, content, and tracked events.

Conversion signals

Form activity, lead-generation events, and other key actions where measurement is available.

Organic visibility

Keyword movement, gains, declines, emerging opportunities, and terms moving closer to stronger positions.

Email engagement

Opens, clicks, unsubscribes, follow-up performance, and audiences showing stronger interest.

Page performance

High-traffic pages, changing user activity, and content or landing pages worth improving or expanding.

Decision making

Separate the signal from the story.

I start with the observed change, add context, then decide whether the next step is to investigate, repeat, improve, or test something new.

  • What changed? Identify the actual movement in traffic, rankings, engagement, or campaign performance.
  • Why might it matter? Connect the change to customer behavior and business priorities.
  • What should happen next? Turn the finding into a practical action or question for the team.

Client-specific reporting stays private. The portfolio focuses on the analysis approach and the kinds of decisions I support.
