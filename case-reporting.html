My process

Check before drawing a conclusion.

  1. Confirm the comparison

    Use the right dates, source and reporting period. Check whether a follow-up reached a different audience.

  2. Look beyond one metric

    Read clicks, website engagement and list-health signals together. An open or a click is not a confirmed sale.

  3. Investigate unusual changes

    Check possible traffic anomalies, campaign activity and tracking gaps before explaining a large movement.

  4. Write the decision

    Keep the client update short and connect each finding to an appropriate next step.

Result

Readable reporting and a usable handoff.

I prepare comparison tables, written observations and short summary emails. I also review reports for date accuracy, relevant keywords, useful sources and conclusions supported by the figures.
