Give search intent a useful destination.

I turn keyword research into content direction by checking the business offer, the search intent and the location before choosing a target page.

Context

A keyword list needs a purpose.

A long export does not explain which searches fit the business or where each topic belongs. Similar phrases can create repetitive page ideas if intent is not checked first.

I review the service and coverage area, group related searches, and connect each topic to a useful destination.

My decisions

Fit, intent, destination.

  1. Check business fit

    Keep terms that match actual services and approved locations. Query relevance matters before a term becomes a target.

  2. Group by intent

    Separate core services, supporting topics and local searches. Review search results before treating similar phrases as different page opportunities.

  3. Map the page

    Connect each focus keyword to a relevant service page or article. Use the mapping to guide content and on-page optimization.

  4. Support implementation

    Give content and citation work a consistent keyword and URL reference. Recheck the destination when content changes.
