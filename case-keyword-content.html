Search strategy + content growth

Keyword Strategy & Content Operations

I use search demand, audience context, competitor activity, and business priorities to decide where a website can improve visibility and what content deserves attention first.

How I approach the work

  • Understand the business, audience, services, locations, and goals.
  • Research keyword opportunities and related demand.
  • Compare relevance, search volume, competition, and business value.
  • Group and prioritize terms around pages and topics.
  • Use findings to guide content, internal links, and website updates.
  • Review final content for clarity, accuracy, and usefulness.
Keyword work

Move from search data to priorities.

Keyword research becomes useful when it helps decide what to build, improve, combine, or deprioritize.

Content strategy

Research before writing.

Content planning starts with the audience, the question they are trying to answer, the business goal, and the role of the page in the wider site.

  1. Clarify the goal: what should the page help the business and the customer accomplish?
  2. Choose the opportunity: select keywords and topics that fit the service, audience, and realistic growth potential.
  3. Build the structure: organize the page around useful information, supporting topics, and natural internal links.
  4. Create and refine: draft efficiently, then review for factual accuracy, clarity, tone, keyword use, and usability.
  5. Connect the page: make sure the content supports the rest of the site and has a clear next step.

Growth questions I use

  • Where is the business already visible?
  • Which important services or topics are underrepresented?
  • What are competitors covering that the site is missing?
  • Which pages could gain from stronger internal links?
  • Which opportunities are closest to commercial value?
Competitor research

Turn comparison into action.

I compare competitors to find practical opportunities in page coverage, local reach, content depth, backlinks, visibility, and user experience.

Coverage gaps

Identify services, locations, questions, and topics competitors cover more effectively.

Visibility gaps

Compare keyword and backlink patterns to find areas where the business can strengthen its presence.

Growth priorities

Translate research into a short list of pages, links, content, or improvements worth acting on next.
