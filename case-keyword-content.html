Professional work · Keyword strategy

Choosing what deserves a page.

I turn keyword research into a usable content direction by checking the service, search intent and target location.

My role
Keyword research, categorization and content coordination
Output
Keyword groups and keyword-to-page mapping

The challenge

A keyword list needs a purpose.

A long export does not explain which searches fit the business or where each topic belongs. Similar phrases can also lead to repetitive content if they are treated as separate opportunities without checking intent.

I begin with the business’s services and coverage area, then use research to decide which terms to keep, group and map.

My contribution

Connect the research to the content.

  1. Check fit

    Confirm the service, geography and audience before selecting keywords.

  2. Group related searches

    Separate core services, supporting topics and local intent. Review the search results before treating similar phrases as different pages.

  3. Map the destination

    Connect each focus keyword to the relevant service page or published article.

  4. Support the handoff

    Give content and citation work a consistent keyword and URL reference.

Result

Research with a destination.

The work turns a raw keyword list into categories that can support a content plan and keyword-to-URL mapping. It also gives recurring off-page work a relevant target instead of an arbitrary link.

My AI-search work is research into how useful content and accurate information can support discovery. I apply that thinking without claiming specialist AI-ranking results.
