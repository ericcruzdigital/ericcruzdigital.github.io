Keyword Research & Content Operations

My workflow starts with the business, service area, audience, and existing site — then uses keyword data and research to decide what content should be created or improved.

Core workflow

  • Review services, location, audience, and constraints.
  • Research seed terms and keyword ideas in Ubersuggest.
  • Balance relevance, search volume, and difficulty.
  • Organize and track selected terms.
  • Use research to guide content, internal links, and updates.
  • Review AI-assisted drafts manually before finalization.

Content research

Research before drafting.

Content work is stronger when the audience, source material, facts, and target query are defined before writing begins.

Method

How I approach the work.

  1. Understand the client: services, location, audience, goals, restrictions, and existing website.
  2. Build the keyword set: use service/location combinations or non-geo seed terms, then evaluate relevance and opportunity.
  3. Organize the data: selected keywords are categorized, tracked, and moved into a central working sheet.
  4. Research evidence: content facts should be specific, sourced, and useful to the target audience.
  5. Draft with structure: AI can help accelerate drafting, but it does not replace research, review, or judgment.
  6. Finalize and QA: review the copy for relevance, consistency, keyword use, clarity, and readiness for publishing.

Competitor analysis

Turn competitor research into an action list.

A documented local SEO competitor analysis in my personal work files compares verified competitors across indexing/content structure, backlink and keyword gaps, service-page depth, location coverage, blog/media footprint, and engagement features.

  • Confirm that the businesses are genuine search competitors, not just large brands in the same industry.
  • Compare page architecture, service depth, local pages, keyword visibility, and backlink profile.
  • Identify missing service pages, local-content opportunities, link gaps, and multimedia/engagement gaps.
  • Translate the findings into prioritized recommendations instead of stopping at a comparison table.
