Competitor analysis

Compare the gaps that can actually change the plan.

In competitor work, I look beyond a simple ranking comparison. I compare service-page depth, location coverage, content footprint, backlinks, authority signals, offers, media, and the kinds of pages competitors use to capture demand.

The useful output is a roadmap: which pages are missing, which topics need more depth, where local relevance is weak, what authority-building opportunities exist, and what should be prioritized first.

AI-assisted discovery

Modern search adds new surfaces, not a separate shortcut.

I have researched forum and community visibility, entity consistency, factual structured answers, external mentions, and how useful discussions can support discovery in AI-assisted search environments.

My practical approach is to keep the same standard: useful information, clear entities, credible sourcing, strong website content, authentic off-site visibility, and measurement where the platform makes it available.

Content

Research before drafting.

Content planning starts with the audience question, the search opportunity, the business goal, and the job the page needs to do. I use keyword and competitor research to shape the brief, then review the finished work for clarity, factual accuracy, search intent, useful internal links, and a clear next step.
