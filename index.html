How I approach search now

Strong fundamentals, updated for AI-era search.

I focus on whether a site can be found, understood, trusted, and improved using real performance data.

Technical clarity

Investigate indexing and visibility issues, review site structure and audit output, and separate technical problems from content or demand problems before prescribing a fix.

Intent + useful content

Use keyword, audience, competitor, service, and location context to decide what deserves a page, what should be improved, and how internal links should support the journey.

Measurement that explains why

Use Search Console, GA4, ranking data, and campaign context to explain movement and choose the next action, not just report a dashboard.

AI search without gimmicks

Treat AI-powered discovery as part of search: crawlable pages, original useful information, clear entities, real authority signals, and measurable visibility.
