Search in 2026

The channel is changing. The fundamentals still have to work.

My approach is to adapt to new search behavior without chasing shortcuts.

01

Make the site technically clear

Important content should be crawlable, indexable, easy to navigate, and connected through a sensible site structure.

02

Create content worth retrieving

Useful search content needs specificity, experience, evidence, and a reason to exist beyond repeating what is already everywhere else.

03

Think beyond ten blue links

Visibility now spans local results, websites, forums, images, video, AI-generated answers, and the wider web signals that help people discover a brand.

04

Measure the business signal

Rankings matter, but the useful question is what changed in visibility, engagement, leads, conversion paths, and the next decision.
