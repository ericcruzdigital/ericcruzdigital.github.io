From research to approved publication.

Tools & working context

Context

Content needs a clear path to approval.

In previous agency work, I helped prepare blog material and move it through client review before publication. The work combined research and content preparation with communication, revisions and website publishing.

My contribution covered those stages within a wider team workflow. Authorship and strategy responsibilities varied by assignment.

My contribution

Research, review, revise and publish.

  1. Research the topic

    Research content relevant to the assignment and prepare useful background for the blog material.

  2. Prepare the material

    Organize and prepare content for client review, working within the assignment and available source material.

  3. Communicate for review

    Share material with the client and follow up on feedback or approval.

  4. Implement revisions

    Apply the requested changes and return the revised material for review where needed.

  5. Confirm approval

    Use client approval as the point for moving the content to publication.

  6. Publish the approved post

    Publish the approved blog content on the client website through its CMS workflow.
