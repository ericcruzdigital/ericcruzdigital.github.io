Paid search

Google Ads Campaign Management

My Google Ads work connects campaign structure, keyword intent, local targeting, landing-page messaging, conversion actions, and reporting instead of treating the ad account as a standalone channel.

One hands-on campaign involved a local wellness service business. Client details are removed here, but the work included campaign planning, keyword categorization, negative-keyword control, landing-page preparation, form and phone conversion paths, performance review, and reporting.

Campaign strategy

Start with the customer and the conversion.

The campaign plan begins with what the business is selling, where it serves customers, what searchers actually mean, and which actions count as a lead.

Campaign structure

Search campaign structure organized around specific services, location intent, ad groups, relevant landing pages, and useful ad assets.

Keyword strategy

Keywords are sorted by commercial intent, location intent, competitor relevance, research intent, and negative-keyword risk before they are used in the campaign.

Conversion path

Ads connect to focused landing pages with a clear offer, strong service relevance, phone and form actions, and a thank-you path that supports measurement.

Execution

What I worked on.

  • Research: service, location, customer intent, search terms, competitor language, and negative-keyword risks.
  • Campaign setup: search-focused campaign planning, service-led ad groups, geographic targeting, bidding/budget considerations, and assets.
  • Keyword organization: high-intent terms, location terms, competitor terms, research queries, and exclusions.
  • Landing page: messaging aligned to the ad promise, service benefits, technology/service details, hero form, phone CTA, FAQs, and thank-you content.
  • Measurement: call and form conversion actions, campaign-performance review, and reporting.
  • Optimization: search-term review, negative keywords, CTR/CPC monitoring, conversion review, ad-copy iteration, and budget prioritization.

How SEO experience helps paid search

  • Search-intent analysis makes keyword selection more precise.
  • Content experience improves message match between query, ad, and landing page.
  • Analytics/reporting experience helps separate activity from useful business signals.
  • Operational discipline keeps recurring review and optimization work organized.
Reporting

Use performance to decide what changes next.

I review paid-search performance in context: traffic quality, CTR, CPC, conversions, landing-page alignment, keyword relevance, and opportunities to reduce wasted spend.
