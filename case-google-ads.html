Campaign setup

Start with what the customer is trying to do.

Campaign structure

Organize search campaigns around clear services and intent instead of mixing unrelated offers into the same ad group.

Keyword intent

Separate high-intent service queries, location queries, competitor terms, research terms, and keywords that should be excluded.

Local targeting

Match the campaign to the service area and avoid paying for traffic outside the locations the business can realistically serve.

Ad-to-page match

Keep the search term, ad promise, landing-page headline, service details, and call to action aligned.

Landing page

The click only matters if the next step is clear.

For a local wellness campaign, I worked through the offer, service details, keyword groups, landing-page content, phone and form actions, FAQ content, and thank-you-page flow. The point was not simply to generate clicks, but to make the path from search to lead as clear as possible.

  • One focused offer instead of a general homepage.
  • Headline and supporting copy matched to the service being advertised.
  • Form placed where a ready prospect can act quickly.
  • Phone call path kept visible for people who prefer to speak directly.
  • Negative keywords used to reduce irrelevant research, shopping, job, and unrelated-treatment traffic.
