Professional work · Google Ads

A clearer path from search to enquiry.

I have managed Google Ads campaigns and worked on the planning that connects a service offer to its keywords, audience and landing page.

My role
Campaign management, keyword planning and landing-page content
Output
Campaign briefs and service-focused page content

The challenge

Keep the campaign focused.

Service campaigns need a clear offer and a relevant audience. Broad or unrelated searches can pull attention away from the people looking for that service.

My planning materials start with the client’s service, coverage area and desired enquiry, then connect those decisions to the campaign and landing page.
