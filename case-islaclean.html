IslaClean Palawan

I created IslaClean to turn keyword strategy into a complete local-service customer journey: focused service pages, local relevance, internal links, clear calls to action, and CTA and form actions prepared for tracking.

Open live website ↗About the project

Independent portfolio project created for hands-on SEO and website development.

What I built

  • 10-page responsive HTML/CSS/JavaScript website.
  • Dedicated pages for routine cleaning, deep cleaning, move-in/out cleaning, and local service coverage.
  • Page-specific titles, descriptions, headings, and canonical URLs.
  • Internal links connecting services, local content, blog content, and contact paths.
  • CTA and form interactions prepared for event tracking.
  • A simple, fast front end that is easy to inspect and improve.
Search strategy

Matching pages to what customers are looking for.

Instead of placing every service on one page, the site gives important customer needs their own destination and a clear path toward contact.

Service demand

Focused service pages

House Cleaning, Deep Cleaning, and Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning each address a distinct need and create more room for relevant search coverage.

Local growth

Puerto Princesa relevance

A dedicated service-area page strengthens local context and gives service pages a natural local destination to link to.

Content growth

Helpful informational content

The blog adds useful cleaning content that can support discovery, internal links, topical depth, and future content expansion.

Growth thinking

More than ranking a page.

The project connects four parts of the customer journey: being found, understanding the service, reaching a clear call to action, and preparing the site for measurement.

  • Visibility: organize pages around meaningful services and local demand.
  • Relevance: make each page useful for a specific customer need.
  • Conversion path: keep calls to action and contact routes easy to find.
  • Measurement: prepare CTA and form interactions for future analytics testing.
