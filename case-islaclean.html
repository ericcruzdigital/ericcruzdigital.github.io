What I built

A real site instead of another hypothetical SEO document.

Service architecture

Separate pages for distinct services so search intent and customer needs are not forced into one generic page.

Local relevance

Service-area structure and Puerto Princesa context to support a local-service search experience.

On-page SEO

Titles, descriptions, headings, canonical URLs, internal links, and content organized around the purpose of each page.

Conversion path

Clear calls to action and contact routes, with key interactions prepared for future analytics tracking.

Why it matters

It lets me test the whole search journey.

The project connects keyword thinking to an actual website: page selection, site architecture, content, local context, internal links, technical checks, and the next step a visitor should take.

It also gives me a controlled environment for future work such as Search Console analysis, event verification, content expansion, technical auditing, and conversion testing as real data becomes available.
