Professional work · SEO operations & reporting

Keeping campaigns on course.

My recurring work combines performance review, SEO updates, local and off-page work, reporting and team coordination.

My role
Review, reporting, quality checks and coordination
Output
Campaign updates and actionable follow-ups

The challenge

Different accounts. Clear priorities.

Recurring campaigns need consistent reviews without losing sight of their individual services, locations and current issues. A change in traffic or rankings needs an explanation before it becomes a recommendation.

I review the available data, check the campaign scope and prepare an update that explains what changed and what should happen next.

My contribution

Make the findings usable.

  1. Review the period

    Check the reporting dates, keyword tracking, website signals and relevant email results.

  2. Investigate changes

    Check indexing, target pages, location relevance and possible causes when a campaign needs attention.

  3. Coordinate the follow-up

    Record the next action, owner and blocker. Review citation records before repeating work.

  4. Prepare the update

    Present the findings in a readable report and short summary for the client or team.
