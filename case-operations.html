Off-page SEO

A system for citations, backlinks, and authority.

My off-page process is built around relevance, consistency, verification, and distribution rather than creating links without a reason.

  • Research: identify directories, business listings, profiles, forums, Q&A opportunities, resource pages, and other sources that fit the business.
  • Prioritize: consider authority, category fit, local relevance, trust, and whether the placement supports the campaign goal.
  • Execute: create or claim listings, maintain consistent business information, verify accounts, and document status.
  • QA: countercheck created citations, flag broken or low-value sources, and prevent duplicate or inconsistent work.
  • Improve: research new sources and update the process when search behavior, platform quality, or AI-search visibility changes.

AI search & community visibility

Extend authority beyond traditional rankings.

I have also researched how useful forum discussions, structured answers, entity consistency, credible sourcing, and website content can reinforce visibility in AI-assisted search environments.

  • Map relevant communities and recurring questions.
  • Prioritize useful, factual contributions over promotional posting.
  • Use clear entities, supporting sources, and structured explanations.
  • Repurpose strong questions and discussions into deeper website content.
  • Track engagement, referral visibility, mentions, and AI-search presence where practical.
