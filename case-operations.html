Modern search

Traditional SEO is still the base. Discovery is getting broader.

I also research how forums, credible third-party mentions, structured answers, entity consistency, images, and useful website content can influence discovery beyond a standard results page.

I do not treat AI-search visibility as a separate bag of tricks. The practical focus is still useful content, technical clarity, trustworthy sources, clear entities, and measuring where visibility is actually appearing.
