Professional work · Operations

Keep recurring work moving.

A campaign review is useful when someone can act on it. My operations work connects SEO findings, content tasks, documentation and follow-ups across remote workflows.

My role
Campaign coordination, process documentation and quality review
Output
Recurring updates, process guides, onboarding materials and follow-up actions
Tools & working context
Basecamp, Google Docs and Sheets, Slack, ClickUp; Otter.ai for meeting notes

Context

Many tasks. One clear handoff.

Recurring SEO work involves different services, locations, reporting dates and unresolved inputs. A task can appear active while its owner, evidence or next step remains unclear.

I review the campaign scope, organize content, citation and press release tasks, and record the action needed. I support delegation and quality review, keeping the source and status visible for the next person.

My contribution

Make the workflow repeatable.

  1. Review the scope

    Check the service, location, target keyword or URL, reporting dates and required output before coordinating follow-up work.

  2. Document the process

    Prepare process guides and onboarding materials that explain the task, source references and checks required before handoff.

  3. Coordinate the follow-up

    Track the action, owner, status and blocker. Surface missing information so the next step can be resolved.

  4. Review the deliverable

    Check date consistency, keyword and URL relevance, duplicate citation records and whether the update is supported by its sources.

Documentation & quality

A short review before delivery.

  • Confirm the task scope and source material.
  • Use the correct dates, approved services and relevant locations.
  • Check the target keyword, destination URL and completed output.
  • Record unresolved inputs and the next action.
  • Give the next person enough context to continue the work.

This is a public summary of my review approach. Private training and client documents are not included.
