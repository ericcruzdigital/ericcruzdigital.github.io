Professional workflow

Off-Page SEO, Email & Digital Operations

My digital work is not limited to research. A large part of the role is operational: keep client data accurate, execute recurring tasks, track status, review output, solve blockers, maintain documentation, and make handoffs easy for the next person.

Operations responsibilities

  • Citation/backlink and SEO-update management.
  • Press-release workflow coordination.
  • Monthly reviews and report generation.
  • Email marketing and weekly stats updates.
  • Website content updates.
  • Team onboarding and training guides/videos.
  • Task delegation, research support, and file organization.
Off-page SEO

Structured citation and backlink work.

The workflow is designed around consistency and traceability rather than blindly creating links.

Business-data consistency

Use centralized client data for name, address, phone, website, categories, and required business details so listings stay consistent.

Source research

Research relevant directory, profile, local, niche, community, and content/outreach opportunities instead of relying on one source list.

Tracking & verification

Record submissions, status, verification, notes, non-working sites, and completion progress in the central tracker.

Problem solving

Investigate how unfamiliar sites work, choose appropriate categories, retry recoverable errors, and communicate real blockers with evidence.

QA & security

Check accuracy, use approved account practices, and keep work logs organized so another team member can understand the current state.

Process improvement

Document better sources, workflow improvements, recurring issues, and updated guidance so future work becomes easier and more consistent.

Email marketing

Campaign support and performance review.

My email responsibilities include newsletters, follow-up sequences, list work, scheduling, campaign-stat updates, and summarizing engagement for the team.

  • Prepare or review newsletter and follow-up content.
  • Maintain consistency in tone and branding.
  • Support list cleanup and audience organization.
  • Review recipients, opens, clicks, unsubscribes, and campaign comparisons.
  • Identify engaged segments or follow-up opportunities.
  • Connect email reporting with broader marketing reports where relevant.
Public portfolio pages do not reproduce individual subscriber names, email addresses, or private lead lists from work files.
